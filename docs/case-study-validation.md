## 1. Evidence Governance Principles

In accordance with Section 1 and Section 8 of the assignment brief:
1. **Never manufacture client statistics, testimonials, approvals, or before-and-after imagery.**
2. **Preserve conflicting source values exactly as recorded in the Growth Deck; do not silently 'reconcile' them.**
3. **Every unverified metric or ambiguous currency denominator is explicitly flagged with an internal `NEEDS VALIDATION` status.**
4. **All data used in public marketing collateral must trace directly to verifiable source documentation.**

## 4. Future Case-Study Pipeline: Data Governance & Approval Checklist

To expand Growify's proof portfolio without manufacturing unverified claims, the following six case-study themes are pre-structured with their exact data requirements:

### Theme 1: Luxury Customer Acquisition (Meta & Google at Scale)
- **Target Brand:** High-AOV Indian Couture & Heritage Bridal (₹85,000+ AOV).
- **Baseline Metrics Required:** Pre-intervention Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), blended ROAS, monthly ad spend.
- **Post-Intervention Metrics:** 12-month trailing blended ROAS, first-party contribution margin, LTV at 90/180 days.
- **Attribution Methodology:** First-party server-side GA4 Data-Driven Attribution combined with post-purchase survey validation.
- **Approvals Required:** Brand CMO written sign-off, Spend confidentiality waiver, Creative ad asset usage release.

### Theme 2: Shopify Plus Redesign & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
- **Target Brand:** Contemporary Designer Pret & Fine Jewellery.
- **Baseline Metrics Required:** Mobile conversion rate by device, bounce rate, checkout abandonment percentage, mobile LCP speed.
- **Post-Intervention Metrics:** Uplift in mobile checkout completion (+35% target), sub-2.2s Largest Contentful Paint.
- **Attribution Methodology:** Pre/post A/B testing cohort comparison over equivalent seasonal calendar quarters.
- **Approvals Required:** Technical Lead review, Client Design Director approval.

### Theme 3: International D2C Expansion (Cross-Border Commerce)
- **Target Brand:** Indo-Western Luxury Pret & Bridal Accessories.
- **Baseline Metrics Required:** Domestic vs international revenue ratio, cross-border shipping SLA, international return rate.
- **Post-Intervention Metrics:** International GMV growth (from 18% to 55%), landed-cost duty conversion lift.
- **Attribution Methodology:** Shopify Markets regional segmentation mapped to foreign currency bank remittances.
- **Approvals Required:** CFO revenue disclosure clearance, Logistics partner SLA verification.

### Theme 4: WhatsApp & Email Lifecycle Retention
- **Target Brand:** Designer Footwear, Pret & Luxury Gifting.
- **Baseline Metrics Required:** 30-day and 90-day repeat purchase rate, email open rates, revenue per recipient.
- **Post-Intervention Metrics:** 38%+ monthly GMV generated from owned channels, WhatsApp read rate >90%, zero spam flags.
- **Attribution Methodology:** Last-click owned-channel attribution with 5-day attribution window and coupon code redemption mapping.
- **Approvals Required:** Retention Marketing Lead, Client CRM Database Administrator.

### Theme 5: Organic Search Authority & High-Intent Fashion SEO
- **Target Brand:** Heritage Bridalwear & Bespoke Menswear.
- **Baseline Metrics Required:** Organic non-branded search impressions, top-3 keyword rankings, organic revenue contribution.
- **Post-Intervention Metrics:** Non-branded organic clicks (+140% target), rankings for high-intent bridal keywords.
- **Attribution Methodology:** Google Search Console query logs reconciled with GA4 First-User Acquisition models.
- **Approvals Required:** SEO Strategy Lead sign-off, Brand Editorial Director alignment.

### Theme 6: AI-Enabled Sales Enablement (Lookify + 18-Hour Concierge Desk)
- **Target Brand:** High-Ticket Custom Measurement Couture.
- **Baseline Metrics Required:** Overseas consultation-to-order closure rate, virtual try-on engagement volume.
- **Post-Intervention Metrics:** Add-to-cart lift on try-on sessions (+38%), stylist WhatsApp closure rate, fit satisfaction score.
- **Attribution Methodology:** Lookify session user IDs linked to custom assisted-checkout payment URLs.
- **Approvals Required:** Lookify Product Lead, Client Boutique Operations Head.
